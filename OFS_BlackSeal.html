To Whom It May Concern

The Order of the Fallen Star community has chosen to continue while cutting out the systems and people who sustained it.

The bots, tools, records, workflows, and infrastructure behind that community were built, hosted, maintained, and improved through hundreds of hours of real labor, personal time, and cost.

When support was requested to continue that work, the response was not support. It was replacement, replication, and removal.

This website is no longer operated for that Discord community, nor should it be treated as its property, voice, or archive.

The work preserved here remains with those who built it.

Let this stand as a warning: be careful where you place your trust, your labor, and your loyalty.

Support the people who support you.

That is all.
